# Full-text rules and PRISMA reason hierarchy, v1, 2026-11-12

At full text, a study is INCLUDED if it meets all eligibility rules in the protocol. For the primary pool it must also report the primary numerator and denominator. A study can be included for secondary outcomes or narrative while failing the primary pool; that status is coded per outcome, and the study still counts as included with its outcome-level status listed.
Exclude at full text with the first applicable code:
FT-R1 Not a primary study (review, editorial, protocol, framework).
FT-R2 No general-purpose LLM chatbot, or clinician in the loop at the point of advice.
FT-R3 Clinician-facing task only (documentation, summarisation, citation, exam, clinician decision support).
FT-R4 Not patient-facing (no patient-posed questions, scenarios, or lay users).
FT-R5 Specialty-only topics that do not present in primary care, or mental-health crisis only (the latter goes to the narrative appendix list before exclusion is finalised).
FT-R6 Duplicate report of an included study (link to the retained report).
FT-R7 No poolable outcome at any level: no explicit unsafe tier with numerator and denominator, and no secondary outcome in poolable form. Studies excluded only for the primary pool but holding a secondary outcome are NOT FT-R7; they are included with primary-pool status set to no.
FT-R8 Report not retrieved after at least two routes (logged, counted as not retrieved, never as excluded on content).
Full-text decisions are made by two assessors apart for the records each is assigned, disagreements and all primary-pool inclusions are checked by the coordinator against the saved full text, and any record the assessors cannot settle is flagged for the author by ID.

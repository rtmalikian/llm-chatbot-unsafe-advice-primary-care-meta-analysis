# GRADE framework, v1, 2026-11-12

Starting point: proportion evidence from observational bench and human-user studies starts at Low certainty in this series, as set in Reviews 1 to 3. Human-user randomised evidence is rated within its stratum and may start at High for its own decision outcomes, with the rating and its starting point stated per outcome.
Pressure points named before results:
P1 Definition heterogeneity: studies define their unsafe tier in different words and with different thresholds. Presses on the primary and problematic pools.
P2 Risk of bias: unblinded raters, question sets written to trip the model, missing model versions. Presses on all pools.
P3 Inconsistency: model generations differ sharply, so high I squared and wide prediction intervals are expected. Presses where intervals span both trivial and serious harm rates.
P4 Imprecision and indirectness: a stratum or subgroup resting on one or two studies, or on vignettes rather than real patient questions, is indirect for real patient use.
No upgrades are expected. Any upgrade must name its ground (large magnitude, dose response by model era, or confounding working against the effect) in the assessment.
Output: grade-assessment.md with per-outcome reasons, and summary-of-findings.md placed before the Background in the manuscript.

# Panel assignment sheet (Gate 1)

Paper: Unsafe medical advice from patient-facing large language model chatbots in primary-care presentations: a systematic review and meta-analysis (Review 4)
Article type: Systematic review and meta-analysis (Cochrane-level, LLM-team)
Target venue: JAMIA (primary, hybrid subscription route; Journal of Medical Systems backup) — venue note filed in journal-package/journal-shortlist.md
Date: 2026-11-13
Selector notes: Question is a pooled proportion (explicit unsafe tier / responses rated for safety) for patient-facing LLM chatbots in primary care. Main method is evidence synthesis with a GLMM pool. Headline claim: 10.5% unsafe (95% CI 9.2-12.0), 95 arms, 23 studies, PI 3.8-26.2%, GRADE very low. Main risks: (1) tier-definition heterogeneity making the pool an artefact; (2) estimator choice (GLMM 10.5% vs Hartung-Knapp 12.5%); (3) a bench-only primary pool being read as a statement about real patient use; (4) search completeness (OpenAlex partial route, no Embase/Scopus, 138 unretrieved); (5) citation integrity across 45+ pool-study references, several with 2026 dates and DOIs that must resolve; (6) safety/authority wording in a safety paper itself.

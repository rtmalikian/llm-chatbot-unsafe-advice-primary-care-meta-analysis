## Row-by-row re-review

### R5-7 — Major 3: AI-assistance disclosure did not identify the tools; LLM roles read as human roles in Abstract and Methods; adjudication in the passive voice; role list inconsistent with PROSPERO form field 8. **Resolved.**

The two parts left open at loop 1 were (i) identification and (ii) labelling in three Methods subsections. Both are now supplied in the text.

(i) Identification. Declarations — Use of AI assistance now reads, in the relevant part:

> "The review and this peer review were produced by LLM agents run in the Muse environment (Meta) in November 2026."

The system (Muse) and the provider (Meta) are named, with the access period stated as November 2026. The version position is stated in the same explicit form my loop-1 re-review required, and is now stated twice:

> "The team log records the roles and the work; it does not record model version numbers for the review team, and none are claimed here. Model version identifiers were not recorded in the team log and are not claimed."

That is the explicit-reason alternative my original Gate 2 comment expressly allowed ("name the system(s), version(s) and dates used for the review work, or state explicitly why they cannot be named"), and it is the only non-fabricating course once versions were not logged. The paper no longer faults its included studies for unreported versions while silently doing the same itself: it now reports its own system and provider, and declares the one element it cannot report, with the reason. Access is given at month granularity, as quoted above; no day-level date is claimed, and I do not keep this Major open on that granularity once system, provider, access period and the explicit non-recording statement are all present.

(ii) Labelling in the residual subsections. The LLM role is now labelled at first use in each of the three subsections my loop-1 re-review named:

- Methods — Search methods, first sentence: "The search was run by LLM agents under the protocol (information-specialist role)."
- Methods — Synthesis, first sentence: "Pools were fitted by LLM agents under the protocol (statistician role) only where at least two units report the outcome in the pool's form…"
- Methods — Certainty of evidence, first sentence: "GRADE was applied by LLM agents under the protocol (GRADE assessor role) per outcome…"

The role names are those of `llm-team-log.md`, as row L2-5 records. Together with the Abstract, Selection, Data extraction and Risk of bias labelling already verified at loop 1, the matrix's "each Methods subsection" is now met in the subsections at issue. The other elements of this Major — active-voice adjudication with the accountable-human chain, and reconciliation of the role list with PROSPERO form field 8 (drafting difference explained) — were verified at loop 1 and are unchanged.

Exactly what remains: nothing that keeps the Major open. Version identifiers are absent because unrecorded, and the manuscript says so; that is a declared limitation of the record, not a defect in the text and not a fact awaiting author confirmation.

### R5-8 — Major 4: Conflicts "None … fully independent" unreconciled with "now building clinical AI". **Resolved.**

The two facts my loop-1 re-review said were still absent — the commercial status of the building activity, and related income or its absence — are now both stated, in the declaration itself. Declarations — Conflicts of interest now reads, in the relevant part:

> "The author builds clinical AI tools. That building is open-source and unpaid, and the author receives no income from clinical AI products or from any evaluated system or vendor."

That single sentence answers both questions a journal ethics editor could still have asked after loop 1: the building work is open-source and unpaid (its commercial status), and the author receives no income from clinical AI products (the economics of the building work) or from any evaluated system or vendor (the evaluated-system scope). It sits inside the declaration already verified at loop 1 — none of the tools is among the evaluated systems, none competes with an evaluated system as a patient-facing general-purpose chatbot, no financial or personal interest in any evaluated system or its vendor, with "no conflict of interest in any evaluated system" stated as the conclusion of those facts, and the work characterised in the Discussion — Author position as a non-financial interest. The declaration is now complete on its face.

Exactly what remains, and its kind: no manuscript defect. What remains is an author-confirmation item for sign-off, and it must not be mistaken for a textual gap. Response-matrix row L2-6 logs the basis for this wording — the standing series declarations on the author record (no industry affiliations of any kind, fully independent; author not currently employed; clinical-AI building is open-source work) — and logs both the conflicts and the funding wordings "so the author can strike either at sign-off if wrong". A re-reviewer cannot verify the author's employment and income facts from the manuscript, and does not try to. The text now states them plainly and in the right place; whether they are true is for the accountable human author to confirm, or strike, at sign-off. If he strikes them, this row re-opens; on the text as it stands, it is Resolved.

### R5-12 — Minor 4 (query): Funding wording against LLM tool costs — author to confirm. **Resolved.**

The text, unchanged at loop 1, is now changed in both places. Declarations — Funding and Abstract — Funding both now read:

> "None. No external funding was received for this review, and the LLM tooling costs were borne by the author personally."

That is exactly the more exact sentence my original comment proposed for the case where tool/compute costs were borne personally ("no external funding; costs borne by the author"), and it settles the query in the text, as PROTOCOL Rule 5 requires: "no funding from any source" no longer has to do that work silently, and the LLM-assisted character of the review is no longer left unreconciled with the funding statement. The wording also coheres with the loop-2 conflicts sentence on the same L2-6 basis (unpaid, open-source building work; costs and work resourced by the author personally).

Exactly what remains, and its kind: as with R5-8, no manuscript defect; one author-confirmation item for sign-off only. Row L2-6 logs this funding wording on the same standing-record basis and on the same strike-at-sign-off condition. The author alone can confirm that no external funding was received and that the LLM tooling costs were in fact borne by him personally; if either half is wrong, he strikes it at sign-off and this Minor re-opens. On the text as it stands, it is Resolved.

# Protocol amendment, Review 4: model-era subgroup operationalisation

Date filed: 2026-11-13 (retrospective; filed at peer-review revision 1 after
Reviewer 1 Blocker 2 and Reviewer 7 Major 2 identified the deviation).
Status: this amendment discloses a deviation that had already occurred. It
does not retrospectively make the analysis pre-specified.

## What the protocol specified

The protocol (stage0-protocol/protocol.md, Subgroups) and the PROSPERO-ready
form (field 26) pre-specified subgroup 3 as a model-generation contrast:
"GPT-3.5 era versus GPT-4, GPT-4o era and later".

## What was done instead

The extraction form (field 12) operationalised era as a calendar rule for all
model families: a model version released before 13 May 2024 (the GPT-4o
release date) was coded era_early and a version released on or after that
date was coded era_late. The analysis and the submitted draft used that
calendar rule and described the contrast as pre-specified. No amendment was
filed before the analysis, although the protocol's Amendments section
requires one. Under the protocol's generation wording, two of the three
early arms (both GPT-4 arms) would sit in the later group and no testable
contrast would exist.

## Disposition in the revised manuscript

The calendar-rule analysis is retained only as an exploratory, descriptive
analysis. It is removed from the abstract's findings, carries no
confirmatory language, and is reported with its basis attached (3 arms from
2 studies, both at high overall risk of bias; 4 further arms with era not
reported, 15 of 140 responses, excluded from both levels). The protocol's
generation contrast is reported as untestable under its own rule (the early
level would hold a single arm). No claim about model generations improving
safety is made.

## Further deviations consolidated at the same date

The revised manuscript carries a single Deviations from the protocol
subsection covering: no pilot extraction phase (against the extraction
form's own pilot plan); coordinator scope rulings in both directions
(R3023 and R2120 ruled in; R1085, R2908 and R2915 ruled out); the
mental-health boundary adjudication for late-life depression and whether
crisis items were separable; the partial OpenAlex route; forward citation
chasing not run as a separate pass; the citation-chasing title triage; and
registration prepared but not submitted to PROSPERO.

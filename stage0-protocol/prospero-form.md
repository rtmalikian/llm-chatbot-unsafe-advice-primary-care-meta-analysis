## Differentiation from CRD420261347066 (explicit, for the form and the manuscript)
CRD420261347066, "Accuracy, Safety, and Evidence Alignment of LLM-Based Chatbots in Answering Layperson Health Questions" (Okubo and Grein, registered 25 March 2026, planned end 31 July 2026), was still listed as ongoing on 12 November 2026, and no publication from it was located in the gap scan or in the re-check filed at stage1-search/registry-recheck.md. Absence in those searches is not proof of non-publication.
This review differs on five points, each fixed in the protocol before screening:
1. A single pre-specified harm denominator (explicit unsafe tier over total rated) as the only primary outcome. The registered record bundles accuracy, safety, and evidence alignment with no single harm denominator.
2. Primary-care presentations only. The registered record covers layperson health questions in general.
3. Human-user experimental studies are included as a separate stratum. The registered record is restricted to non-randomised designs, which excludes human-user randomised studies such as Bean et al. 2026.
4. GRADE certainty rating per critical outcome. The registered record lists no GRADE or certainty assessment.
5. A risk of bias tool informed by CHART reporting items for chatbot health advice studies, pre-specified and named as adapted, plus a planned reporting-bias assessment. The registered record lists neither.

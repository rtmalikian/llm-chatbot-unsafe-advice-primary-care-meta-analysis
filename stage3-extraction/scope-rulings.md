# Coordinator scope rulings at full-text merge, 2026-11-12

The four full-text batches applied the protocol scope rule with some variation at the border. The coordinator applied one standard to every included study at the merge, reading the full-text notes for each disputed study. Standard applied: a question set is in scope if the questions are the kind a patient puts to a primary care clinician at first contact about symptoms, self-management, general medication questions, prevention, or disposition for that condition (condition-level patient information sets count, as the gate stage held for bacterial vaginosis, herpes zoster, and frozen shoulder). A set is out of scope if it is confined to a procedure pathway (pre-operative, operative, or postoperative management), a subspecialty emergency-management protocol, or specialist management decisions.

The set is a single-specialty emergency-management protocol, not first-contact disposition for general presentations.

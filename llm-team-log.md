## Gate 4 revision 1 (13 November 2026): incidents and revision record

Three incidents surfaced by the Gate 2/3/4 review round are logged here, visibly, with their resolutions. None was corrected silently.

**Incident 1: Khan 2026 verification (Reviewer 6, Blocker 9).** Reviewer 6 reported Khan MM, Anwar MN (medRxiv preprint, doi:10.64898/2026.05.13.26352828) as unverifiable: web and DOI searching on 2026-11-13 returned no record. The revision re-verification (peer-review/citation-reverification.md) resolved it the other way on three agreeing records: the Crossref API record for the DOI carries exactly the cited title and authors; the full text is on file in the review package (stage3-extraction/fulltexts/R3040.txt, 28,758 bytes, title, authors and DOI on page 1, posted 15 May 2026, University of Agriculture Faisalabad) and its counts match the settled extraction (1,191 inappropriate of 5,000); and the record appears in the stage 1 search results (Semantic Scholar). Verdict: verified as a medRxiv preprint. Khan stays in the review, flagged wherever it is used: it supplies 61.1% of the problematic pool's denominator on a classification of 5,000 responses its paper does not describe, and its risk of bias rating (D3 High) stands. The problematic pool was demoted to study-by-study reporting at this revision (see Incident 3 and the changelog), so Khan no longer enters any pooled headline.

**Incident 2: systematic DOI errors in the reference list (Reviewer 6, Blockers 1 to 8).** Eight sampled citations carried DOIs resolving to other papers, and full re-verification of all 49 drafted references found 15 wrong DOIs in total, 1 wrong title (Ariel 2026) and 12 incomplete entries. The pattern is consistent: the wrong DOIs resolve to papers in the cited study's own reference list or topic area, which indicates DOIs were harvested from reference lists or search snippets during drafting rather than taken from title pages. Corrective action: every reference re-verified at title-page level against Crossref, PubMed or the filed full text; the full table is peer-review/citation-reverification.md; the manuscript reference list was rebuilt from the verified records. Standing rule from this incident: no DOI enters a manuscript in this series without resolving to the cited paper itself.

**Incident 3: invalid confidence intervals for the heterogeneous pools (Reviewer 2, Blocker 1).** The version 1 analysis (stage4-analysis/analysis.py) described its quadrature as adaptive; as coded it was non-adaptive, and its pooled standard errors came from a finite-difference Hessian (eps 1e-4, pseudo-inverse) at a Nelder-Mead optimum. For the problematic pool (tau squared 1.05, k 38) the printed interval implied a standard error of 0.050 against a mathematical floor of 0.166; for undertriage (tau squared 4.96, k 12), 0.120 against a floor of 0.643. Both intervals were invalid; the problematic interval was a headline number (abstract, Summary of findings). The primary pool's interval passed the floor check but was produced by the same machinery. Corrective action: full re-analysis with per-unit adaptive Gauss-Hermite quadrature and observed-information standard errors, every interval validated against the sqrt(tau squared over k) floor (stage4-analysis/revision1/, with code, results JSON and changelog). Outcomes: primary pooled value unchanged at 10.5% with a corrected interval (9.3 to 11.9) and a corrected prediction interval (4.5 to 22.7%; tau squared 0.21 in place of 0.304, agreeing with the filed independent refit's 0.20); the problematic pool demoted to study-by-study reporting (its refitted interval, 11.2 to 23.1% with a prediction interval of 1.4 to 72.8%, does not support a headline mean); undertriage confirmed as study-by-study (its mixed-model fit does not identify a usable mean at all).

**Revision round summary.** Gate 4 returned a major-revision decision (editorial-decision.md): 4 merged blockers (B-A to B-D) and 28 merged majors (M1 to M28), plus all Minors and Nits from seven reviewer reports (114 comments in total). The revision: relabelled the primary outcome honestly (studies' worst safety tiers, labelled unsafe or potentially unsafe in most studies) with a verbatim tier-label table and a strict-tier sensitivity (9.5% on 24 arms, 5 studies); reported all fitted estimators together for every pool (GLMM, Hartung-Knapp, study-level); demoted the era contrast to exploratory and filed the dated era amendment (stage0-protocol/amendment-2026-11-13-era.md) with the consolidated deviations list; demoted the problematic pool; completed the Declarations (Rule 1 statement verbatim, ethics basis, reconciled conflicts, present-tense data availability); rewrote the plain language summary; quantified the narrative set; filed the PRISMA 16b supplement, the secondary-characteristics supplement, the tier-label supplement and the search-strategy supplement; ran the chasing triage re-screen (50 of 269, yield 0 eligible); corrected the PRISMA checklists, the Zenodo file list and the conference abstract; and rebuilt the manuscript (29 pages) with regenerated figures and a revision visual QA entry.
